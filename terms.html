1. Introduction

Effective date: —

Welcome to Guided Path Noida ("GPN", "we", "us", "our"), a coaching and learning centre based in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India. GPN operates the following services collectively referred to as the "Platform":

  • The GPN Central System accessible at this website

By accessing or using the Platform, you ("you", "user", "student", "parent", "guardian") agree to be bound by these Terms, our Privacy Policy and our Data Protection commitments. If you do not agree, please do not use the Platform.

These Terms are published in accordance with the provisions of the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the rules made thereunder. They constitute a valid electronic record under the applicable laws of India.

2. Terms of Use

2.1 Eligibility

  • Students using the Platform for assessments must be currently enrolled with GPN.
  • Parents/guardians may access their child's records through the Parent View.
  • Access credentials (Student ID, Parent ID, password) are issued by GPN administration only.
  • Users must keep credentials confidential. GPN is not responsible for losses caused by sharing credentials.

2.2 Acceptable Use

You agree not to:

  • Use the Platform for any unlawful purpose
  • Attempt to gain unauthorised access to any part of the Platform
  • Interfere with, disrupt or place undue load on our systems
  • Share, publish, or resell any GPN course materials, booklets, test papers or answers
  • Impersonate another user or submit test answers on behalf of another student
  • Reverse-engineer, scrape or extract bulk data from the Platform

2.3 Test Integrity

  • Each online test is issued with a unique submission code. Submitting a test with another person's code is a violation.
  • Tests have a fixed duration and a two-window system. Attempts made outside valid windows will be rejected.
  • Attempts flagged for suspected malpractice may be voided without refund.
  • Malpractice may lead to permanent suspension of account and programme.

2.4 Fees & Payments

  • Fees for online programmes, offline tuition and digital products are published on the Platform.
  • Fees are due on the dates indicated on invoices or fee-cycle cards.
  • Late payments may attract suspension of access until dues are cleared.
  • All amounts are in Indian Rupees (₹).

2.5 Intellectual Property

  • All course content, booklets, test papers, question banks, videos and materials are the intellectual property of GPN or its licensors.
  • You may use the materials strictly for your own personal learning.
  • You may not reproduce, distribute, upload or sell any part of the materials.
  • Unauthorised use will result in immediate legal action.

2.6 Account Suspension & Termination

GPN reserves the right to suspend or terminate your access at any time if you violate these Terms, if fees remain unpaid, or if your account poses a security risk. Where reasonable, we will notify you before suspension.

2.7 Communication Consent

  • By using the Platform you consent to receive service-related communications from GPN via email, SMS and WhatsApp.
  • These include: welcome messages, test reminders, fee reminders, result notifications and password change confirmations.
  • You may opt out of non-essential communications by contacting us; essential service messages cannot be opted out of.

2.8 Third-Party Services

  • Online tests use Google Forms (Google LLC).
  • Digital files are delivered through our own content delivery network.
  • Email notifications are sent via Google mail services on our behalf.
  • Payments may be collected via UPI/NEFT through regulated payment channels.
  • We are not responsible for outages of third-party services that are outside our control.

2.9 Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, GPN is not liable for indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages arising out of your use of the Platform. Our total liability, in any case, shall not exceed the fees paid by you to GPN in the preceding three (3) months.

3. Privacy Policy

Effective date: —

3.1 Information We Collect

When you use GPN services, we may collect:

  • Identity data: full name, father's/parent's name, gender, date of birth
  • Contact data: phone number, WhatsApp number, email, residential location
  • Academic data: class, board, school, programme enrolments, attendance, test attempts, scores, teacher remarks
  • Financial data: fee cycles, payments, wallet ledger (no card or bank details stored)
  • Technical data: login timestamps, session tokens, IP address recorded for security and fraud prevention
  • Communications: emails and WhatsApp messages exchanged with us

3.2 How We Collect

  • Directly from you or your parent/guardian when you enrol or sign in
  • Automatically when you use the Platform (session logs, activity logs)
  • From our own staff when they record attendance, marks, fees

3.3 How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and personalise the learning experience
  • To deliver tests, evaluate submissions, publish results
  • To process fees and maintain wallet ledgers
  • To send service notifications (test reminders, fee reminders, results)
  • To respond to your queries and provide support
  • To detect and prevent fraud, malpractice or unauthorized access
  • To comply with legal obligations

3.4 How We Share

We do not sell your data. We may share limited data with:

  • Google — for Google Forms-based assessments and email delivery
  • Zoho — for sending emails from our domain
  • Regulatory / law enforcement authorities — only when required by law

3.5 Data Storage & Security

  • Data is stored on Google Workspace services (Sheets, Drive) with access restricted to GPN.
  • Passwords are hashed using a secure one-way algorithm. Plain-text passwords are never stored.
  • Sessions expire automatically after 12 hours.
  • Login attempts are rate-limited to prevent brute-force attacks.
  • Backups are taken periodically to prevent loss.

3.6 Data Retention

  • Active student records are retained while you remain enrolled with GPN.
  • After you leave GPN, your records may be retained for up to 7 years to comply with tax and accounting obligations, after which they are deleted.
  • Session and login-attempt logs are purged automatically after 30 days.

3.7 Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Withdraw consent for optional communications
  • Lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Grievance Officer (Section 7).

4. Data Protection (DPDP Act, 2023)

4.1 Data Fiduciary

For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, GPN acts as a Data Fiduciary in respect of the personal data of students, parents and staff processed through the Platform.

4.2 Lawful Basis

We process your personal data on the following lawful bases:

  • Consent — given by you or, if you are under 18, by your parent/guardian
  • Legitimate use — to deliver enrolled services, prevent fraud, ensure safety
  • Legal obligation — to comply with applicable laws and tax requirements

4.3 Purpose Limitation

We collect data only for the specific, clear and lawful purposes described in Section 3.3. We do not process data beyond those purposes without fresh consent.

4.4 Data Minimisation

We collect only the minimum data necessary for the described purposes. For example, we do not ask for your Aadhaar number, bank details or any sensitive document beyond what is strictly needed.

4.5 Consent Notice

In accordance with Section 5 of the DPDP Act, we provide you a clear notice describing the personal data we seek and the purpose of processing. This notice is given at the point of data collection (enrolment, parent linking, etc.).

4.6 Right to Withdraw Consent

You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting our Grievance Officer. Upon withdrawal, we will cease processing the affected data unless we are required to continue by law.

4.7 Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a personal data breach, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals in accordance with the timelines prescribed under the DPDP Act.

4.8 Cross-Border Transfer

Your data is stored on infrastructure located in India (Google Workspace Asia region, Zoho India). Where any component is processed outside India, we ensure equivalent protection.

4.9 Data Protection Officer

GPN has appointed a Grievance Officer who also acts as the point of contact for data protection matters. See Section 7.

5. Children's Data (Under 18)

Most of our students are under 18 years of age. In accordance with Section 9 of the DPDP Act, 2023:

  • We process children's data only with verifiable consent of a parent or lawful guardian.
  • We do not undertake tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising directed at children.
  • We do not process personal data of children in any manner that is likely to cause a detrimental effect on their well-being.
  • Parents/guardians can view their child's data through the Parent View, request corrections, or request deletion at any time.

Parents can access the Parent View using the Parent ID issued by GPN. If you are a parent and have not received your Parent ID, contact the centre.

6. Refunds & Cancellation

6.1 Coaching Fees

  • Fees once paid are non-refundable for the ongoing fee cycle.
  • Advance fees paid for future cycles may be refunded on written request, minus administrative charges, if the student withdraws before the cycle begins.
  • Refunds for cancelled or discontinued programmes will be processed at the discretion of GPN administration.

6.2 Digital Products

  • Digital products (booklets, PDFs, material packs) are non-refundable once delivered.
  • If a file is corrupted or fails to open within 7 days of purchase, we will replace it at no cost.

6.3 Test Packs and Courses

  • Test packs and courses, once activated, are non-refundable.
  • Unactivated credits remain valid for 12 months from purchase.

6.4 Wallet Balances

  • Wallet balances used to pay for tests are refunded automatically if the test does not proceed due to a technical failure on our side.
  • Wallet balances may be settled on request in case of permanent withdrawal.

6.5 Processing Time

Approved refunds are typically processed within 7–10 working days to the original payment method.
